MirrorHome

Privacy Policy

Effective August 22, 2026

Overview

MirrorHome is designed to discover compatible TVs and cast media over your local network. The app does not require an account and does not include advertising or third-party analytics.

Local network and Wi-Fi

MirrorHome uses local-network access to discover and communicate with TVs. With your permission, it reads the current Wi-Fi name to help confirm that your iPhone and TV are on the same network. This information is used on the device and is not sent to MirrorHome servers.

Photos and videos

Media you select is prepared temporarily on your device and delivered directly to the selected TV over the local network. MirrorHome does not upload your photos or videos to a cloud service. Temporary prepared files are managed by iOS and may remain in the app's temporary storage until the system removes them.

Purchases and app data

Subscriptions are processed by Apple through StoreKit. MirrorHome stores onboarding status and the number of successful free media casts on your device. Apple may process purchase information under Apple's own privacy policy.

Support diagnostics

If you choose Contact Support, MirrorHome prepares an email containing the selected TV brand, detected protocols, iOS version, app version, Wi-Fi status, and recent scan logs. You can review or remove this information before sending it. Nothing is sent automatically.

Children's privacy

MirrorHome is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app or applicable requirements change. The effective date at the top of this page identifies the latest version.

Contact

For privacy questions, email support@mirrorhome.app.
